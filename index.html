Recipe #1

  • Time: 10 minutes
  • Serves: 1 person (or more if you choose to cook multiple servings)
Ingredients Quantity
Maggi Instant Noodles Packet One per serving
Water 1.5 cups per serving
Optional: Vegetables However much you want
Tumeric 1/4 a teaspoon per serving
Optional: Butter However much you want to soften it (however it dulls taste)

Step 1: Pour water into a pot.
Step 2: Put it onto the stove.
Step 3: Turn on the stove to the appropriate setting.
Step 4: Put in the noodles.
Step 5: Put in the masalas/flavor packets.
Step 6: Optional: put in the tumeric.
